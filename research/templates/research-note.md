# Research Note Title

- **ID:** note-YYYY-MM-DD-short-name
- **Status:** exploratory | comparative | hypothesis | result | deprecated
- **Question/scope:** What this note examines and explicitly does not examine.
- **Corpus records:** List stable IDs, such as `src.example`.

## Evidence Basis

For each cited record, identify the inspected file or artifact and whether the observation is source-stated, directly observed, inferred, or unavailable.

## Observations

Record observations separately from interpretation. Keep negative evidence and incompatible assumptions visible.

## Uncertainty

Use `Unknown`, `Unclear`, `Not established`, `Not evaluated`, or `Conflicting evidence`. Do not fill gaps with inference.

## Follow-ups

List only actions that would test, qualify, or falsify the current observations.
