IRIS-X private offline wheelhouse for Linux x86_64, glibc >= 2.28, CPython 3.12.
The original upstream wheel files retain their own licenses and notices.
Extracted license copies and their original archive paths are recorded in license-inventory.json.
Core package versions and hashes are bound to model-source-lock.json; all 52 resolved distributions
are pinned by requirements.lock and wheel-lock.json. Resolve with --no-index --find-links wheels
--require-hashes and the reviewed offline helper in the separately versioned runtime Dataset.
Native Linux CPU installation, imports, tokenizer and header inspection were verified.
No full model load, GPU operation or Kaggle notebook execution has been performed.
