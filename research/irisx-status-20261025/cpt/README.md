## Preserved research findings

The 2026-10-24 07:20 snapshot reported 7,647 text views / 6,702,077 raw-text
tokens, of which 7,567 views / 5,521,209 shifted CPT targets fit context 8192.
Those are **length-eligible research candidates**, not admitted training data
or independent mechanisms. Its base supply was 960 grid instances plus 664
algorithm cases with ten grid and six algorithm templates. Re-expressing a
parent as multiple documents does not create new rules or knowledge.

The 800 official training source parents and 2,573 observed examples were
checked separately. They do not become 800 distinct mechanisms, and their
token totals must not be added to a different snapshot's census without a
single explicit admission manifest. No private query answers are included here.

Later v5 retained earlier bytes and added a reversible grid serialization:
the fixed 80 long traces fell from 1,180,868 to 760,335 shifted targets, but
only **4/80** then fit 8192. It did not admit the other 76 by truncation.
A later finite schedule reported 7,919 eligible rows / 7,630,597 targets;
that is a different stated snapshot/schedule, not a corrected version of the
07:20 census and not observed native exposure.

The source/math study found that full-document CPT was the actual wired pilot
objective. Answer-only and verified solve-trace objectives required separate
versioned bridge admission. Token-share, per-update coefficient share and
gradient contribution are different quantities. Masking CE padding does not
prove the MoE router ignores padding; manual gradient accumulation changes the
effective auxiliary weighting when supervised token counts differ.

Later R1 mask candidates included four logical CPU devices in FP32. Local
auxiliary/gradient successes did not erase the overall **FAIL: 72 passed and 2 failed**
(the two failures were cross-device full-logit tolerance failures); they were not TPU/BF16/35B
acceptance. A finite-domain pre-head gather probe also had a nonfinite-gradient
counterexample and was not adopted as a general replacement.

## What remains closed

No formal objective/mix/LR/warmup/horizon was adopted, no native full-35B
forward/backward/update throughput was established by these CPU reports, and
no model-quality improvement was demonstrated. The six-update horizon reporter
was `NOT_MEASURED / NO_GO`; synthetic receipt tests were not native measurements.

The old 544-case evaluation contract is 35B UTF-8/JSON, including 192 grid
discovery and 352 algorithm cases. It is design-seen and shares low-level DSL
ancestry; whole-family labels do not prove new source/semantic independence.
It cannot be reused unchanged for a 16-symbol 4B model. Gold remains isolated.

CPT data generation, mixture/schedule/objective optimization and formal
training remain paused. This index preserves future recoverability without
reviving a controller, paid task or training permission.
