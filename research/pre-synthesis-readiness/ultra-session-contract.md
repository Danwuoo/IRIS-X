# Single Ultra Session Contract

The work must remain one single continuous Ultra session.

- **Status:** downstream protocol; unexecuted
- **Execution authority:** none; separate owner authorization required
- **Topology:** one continuous GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra session retaining one context
- **Prohibited topology:** independent formulation stages, model-to-model state
  promotions, consolidation runs, or the removed core-formulation program

## Required Inputs

The session begins from the repository state authorized by the owner and reads:

1. `AGENTS.md`, `research/AGENTS.md`, and the current-state authorities;
2. this package's `baseline-manifest.json` and `README.md`;
3. `source-interface.yaml`, `phase-evidence-ledger.yaml`,
   `construct-map.yaml`, `assumption-tension-matrix.md`, and
   `claim-evidence-index.jsonl`;
4. `research/problem-definition.md` as a provisional prior only;
5. primary repository paths whenever a material claim is formulation-defining,
   disputed, surprising, high-priority, or only indirectly represented.

Readiness metadata is navigation, not scientific authority. A current-state or
closure record is decision/synthesis authority within its scope, not independent
support for the direct evidence it summarizes.

## Continuous Analytical Passes

All passes occur in one session and one retained context. They are reasoning
passes, not separate tasks or model executions.

1. **Authority and baseline verification:** confirm current repository state,
   governance, readiness identities, Phase 6 closure, and non-authorization
   boundaries. Record drift before interpreting evidence.
2. **Evidence reconstruction:** traverse high-priority ledger/index entries;
   spot-check and then expand primary authorities for material claims. Preserve
   negative evidence, invalid attempts, and source-dependence.
3. **Construct and tension reconstruction:** compare source-specific meanings,
   units, horizons, ground truth, cost boundaries, and intervention points.
   Leave unjustified normalization unresolved.
4. **Candidate explanandum discovery:** identify several plausible phenomena
   that the corpus could support studying. Do not organize discovery solely
   around the current success-reliability-cost prior or Phase 5 architecture.
5. **Competing formulations:** produce at least three genuinely competing
   formulations using `candidate-formulation-template.md`. Candidates must
   differ in explanandum, causal structure, unit, or discriminating predictions,
   not merely wording or mechanism choice.
6. **Adversarial comparison:** steelman each candidate, search primary
   counterevidence, test double-counting, identify alternative explanations,
   and apply symmetric criteria. Do not use source-count voting.
7. **Adjudication:** select, revise, split, synthesize, or conclude that no
   single unified formulation is justified. A plural research program or no-
   selection result is valid when unification would erase material distinctions.
8. **Theory formation:** only after candidate adjudication, derive any thesis,
   research questions, and falsifiable hypotheses. Preserve unresolved
   alternatives and scope limits.
9. **Experimental program derivation:** derive the smallest discriminating
   evidence program from competing hypotheses and falsifiers, not from a
   feature, mechanism, architecture, or framework roadmap. Do not execute it.
10. **Final audit:** perform provenance, uncertainty, scope, source-dependence,
    and anti-literature-review audits before completing the synthesis.

## Candidate Comparison Requirements

- Apply one explicit comparison rubric symmetrically across candidates.
- Separate empirical support, explanatory reach, discriminability, novelty,
  operationalizability, and unsupported assumptions; do not collapse them into
  an unexplained scalar.
- Recheck important claims against primary paths and label each as
  `source_stated`, `directly_observed`, `inferred`, or `unknown`.
- Treat paper/project pairs, direct results/later closures, and reused
  predecessor concepts as dependent unless independence is established.
- Preserve Batch 2 Result C, Batch 3 Result D, and the later Result A repair as
  different historical events.
- Preserve `stronger_candidate_resolves_miss`,
  `decision_relevant_tradeoff_established`, and
  `bounded_retry_or_recovery_benefit_compared` as `unknown` absent new evidence.

## Required Final Output Sections

1. authority and evidence boundary;
2. reconstructed evidence table with primary paths;
3. construct/tension findings;
4. competing candidate formulations in the neutral template;
5. adversarial comparison and counterevidence;
6. adjudication, including any plural/no-single-formulation result;
7. post-adjudication thesis/RQs/hypotheses, if justified;
8. hypothesis-derived experimental program, unexecuted;
9. contribution-versus-substrate boundary;
10. explicit scope, uncertainty, falsifiers, and unresolved alternatives;
11. anti-literature-review audit.

## Anti-Literature-Review Audit

The final synthesis must answer yes, with evidence, to these checks:

- Does it explain a scientific problem rather than merely catalogue projects?
- Are source summaries subordinated to claims, tensions, mechanisms, and
  discriminating evidence?
- Are direct observations distinguished from source claims and IRIS-X
  inference?
- Are negative evidence and invalid attempts causally relevant rather than
  relegated to footnotes?
- Does the proposed research program distinguish hypotheses rather than build a
  preferred architecture?
- Could the synthesis justifiably conclude plural programs or no unified thesis?

## Stop Boundary

The session ends after the foundational synthesis artifact and its audits. It
must not launch experiments, provider executions, Phase 7, architecture
implementation, or any follow-on model task without new owner authority.
