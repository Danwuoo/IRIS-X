# Assumption and Tension Matrix

Status: **readiness navigation, not synthesis**

This matrix exposes comparison boundaries that a later, separately authorized
single Ultra session must adjudicate. A classification describes the present
relationship between usages; it does not resolve the tension or rank a source.
`Unclear` is retained where the inspected authorities do not support a stronger
classification.

## Cross-cutting double-counting risks

- A paper and its registered implementation may be one research lineage, not
  two independent supports. This especially applies to AFlow, Agent Lightning,
  FrugalGPT, GEPA, NeMo Agent Toolkit, and RouteLLM.
- A direct result, its result note, its closure note, and a later state summary
  are successive representations of one evidence chain.
- Predecessor design claims and IRIS-X syntheses derived from them are not
  independent observations.
- Multiple deterministic fixtures that share generators, validators, or
  acceptance assumptions can share a failure mode even when their run counts
  differ.

## Deliberately unresolved

The matrix does not choose a canonical unit, horizon, evaluator, cost boundary,
or intervention point. It also does not establish that every tension belongs in
one unified thesis; the later synthesis may retain a plural structure.
