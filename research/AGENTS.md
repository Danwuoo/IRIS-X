# Research Zone

Use `research/` for IRIS-X-authored comparative notes, exploratory analysis,
hypotheses, syntheses, and reviewed observations only within an explicitly
authorized research scope. Start from `templates/research-note.md` when useful;
adapt it rather than inventing a document system without need.

The pre-reset material indexed in `HISTORICAL_INDEX.md` is historical lineage,
not active authority. Do not continue or extend it merely because an original
document describes itself as current, active, controlling, or foundational.
New major research programs require explicit owner authorization.

Every substantive claim should state whether it is source-stated, directly
observed locally, inferred, or unknown. Cite stable IDs such as `src.dspy` plus
concrete evidence paths. Preserve uncertainty labels: `Unknown`, `Unclear`,
`Not established`, `Not evaluated`, and `Conflicting evidence`.

Inspect original sources and empirical records before summarizing them. Do not
put runnable experiment inputs, raw outputs, or implementation prototypes here.
Do not edit imported material or historical results to make a new research
interpretation easier.
