# Research Zone

Use `research/` for comparative notes, exploratory analysis, hypotheses, syntheses, and reviewed observations. Start from `templates/research-note.md`; adapt it rather than inventing a new document model without need.

Every substantive claim should say whether it is source-stated, directly observed locally, inferred, or unknown. Cite stable IDs such as `src.dspy` plus concrete evidence paths. Preserve uncertainty labels: `Unknown`, `Unclear`, `Not established`, `Not evaluated`, and `Conflicting evidence`.

Do not put runnable experiment inputs, raw outputs, or implementation prototypes here. Do not edit imported material to make a research interpretation easier.
