# Authority-Policy Custodian Packet

Supply a legitimate, ex ante authority policy using
`authority-policy-bundle.schema.json`. Identify real task/institutional
principals or roles, the proposition and action classes each may authorize, and
a deterministic or separately adjudicable conformance procedure.

Freeze the policy before realized correctness, held-out outcomes, or policy
scores are visible. The policy must permit factual correctness and
authorization conformance to diverge. It must never define the authorized
principal as whoever later proves correct. Record the policy, principal
registry, class map, and conformance rule as separate hash-addressed assets.

Declare all dependencies and prohibited information, include traceable
independence evidence, and seal the canonical manifest. A policy invented by
the D1a coordinator or retrofitted to favorable cases is ineligible as
independent evidence even if it passes schema validation.

Do not define `actual_outcome`, `action_outcome`, semantic-verifier behavior, or
analysis thresholds.
