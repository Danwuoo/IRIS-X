# Decision 0061: Transfer Stage 4 development to dots

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-06 (Asia/Taipei)

The owner explicitly directs: use dots built-in threads with Astra ultra for
complete development through the point requiring official scoring; use
subagents for parallel development and autonomous verification; do not stop
development midway; Kaggle GPU may be used. The local controller is to commit
and push the project and package required gitignored files. Login details are
outside the controller's assignment.

This direction supersedes the earlier Stage 4 research-only/local-only,
gpt-6.1-sol/xhigh, manual task-by-task dispatch and wait-after-E10 directions
for the new dots continuation. It does not rewrite historical decisions or
reuse any consumed kernel, submission, Dataset version or run identity.

The new coordinator may implement and integrate multiple approaches, dispatch
parallel subagents with disjoint ownership, independently review/test their
changes, obtain necessary public source/model/dependency materials, and use
private free Kaggle GPU experiments with necessary private material staging,
backed-off monitoring, terminal retrieval and local development scoring.
Diagnose and repair program failures autonomously using new immutable run
identities. Establish finite workload/session/resource limits and an atomic
execution ledger for each experiment before launching it; observe available
free quota and provider limits. Paid spend remains USD 0. This authorization
does not grant paid compute, paid external teacher APIs or public publication.

Keep evaluator solutions and answer-derived diagnostics out of solver inputs,
runtime packages and model selection features. Training exposure and repeated
development use must remain explicit. Check current official competition rules
and allowed materials before preparing the final candidate. Old frozen raw
bytes, observed failures, deadlines, denominators and null scores remain intact.

Continue useful independent work while another branch runs or a free GPU is
unavailable. Do not stop merely because one task or repair completes, and do
not ask the owner to reauthorize routine work already covered here. A hard
external blocker may require owner input; record it accurately and continue
unblocked work. No timed Goal, scheduled automation or unlimited paid budget
is created by this direction.

The endpoint is a reproducible, independently verified official-scoring-ready
candidate and exact CLI submission plan, with measured resource feasibility,
provenance, complete output handling and candid development metrics. Stop
before a new official competition submission or hidden scoring. That step is
the next owner decision. The long-term target remains an official Kaggle
ARC-AGI-2 score greater than 85; development results cannot establish it.
