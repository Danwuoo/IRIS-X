# Product Non-Goals

Status: current P0 policy, grounded in the non-goals and product principles of
[`PRODUCT_THESIS.md`](../../PRODUCT_THESIS.md) and
[`Decision 0001`](../decisions/0001-adopt-product-oriented-domain-ai-specialization.md).

IRIS-X is not:

- primarily a general-purpose agent framework;
- merely a fine-tuning or model-training platform;
- conventional AutoML limited to selecting or tuning models;
- a benchmark leaderboard that treats maximum score as product success;
- an open-ended research laboratory whose primary output is theory;
- a framework aggregation project where integration is valuable by itself;
- a mandate for multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, retrieval,
  routing, verification, or any other favored technique.

An external framework or technique may become a candidate backend only through
an authorized Engineering Trial and measured product value. Historical presence,
popularity, conceptual appeal, or successful installation is not promotion
evidence.

P0 also does not define Domain Specification or Domain Package contracts,
runtime behavior, a first domain, a provider/model, a backend, a budget, a
deployment form, or a detailed architecture. These exclusions prevent P0 policy
from silently becoming P1 design.
